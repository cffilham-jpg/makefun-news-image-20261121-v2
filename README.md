An entry for the make.fun competition
[`news-image-20261121`](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261121): sum up the
world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image.

The poster is a broadsheet front page. An ink masthead names the day in
large type, and six story panels below it each pair a bold headline with a
short sub-line and a simple pictogram, so the whole day reads at a glance.

## The six stories

1. **Middle East — 3 Houthi missiles shot down over Riyadh.** Saudi air
   defences intercepted three ballistic missiles fired towards the capital
   in the early hours of Saturday, the third straight night of air-raid
   alerts. (Reuters, 21 Nov 2026)
2. **Europe — NATO chief flies to Warsaw.** Secretary-General Mark Rutte
   headed to Poland after Russian missiles and a drone came down in
   south-east Poland during a huge Russian air attack on Ukraine; Prime
   Minister Tusk called the incursion a deliberate provocation.
   (Associated Press; Institute for the Study of War)
3. **Climate — COP31: no deal as talks run on.** The UN climate summit in
   Antalya slipped into overtime with no agreement; the latest draft
   dropped all mention of fossil fuels and a new text was due on Saturday
   morning. (Associated Press; Reuters; UN News)
4. **Hunger — UN cuts food aid target to 91 million.** The World Food
   Programme launched its 2027 appeal for $12.1 billion to assist 91
   million people, down from 110 million, and warned of a third global
   food crisis. (Reuters; WFP)
5. **Tech & markets — Anthropic's $81bn IPO, biggest ever.** Anthropic
   raised $81 billion in the largest stock market debut in history, but
   the blockbuster listing did little to stop the wider AI stock slide.
   (Reuters)
6. **Sport — Harden scores 51 in Cavs win.** James Harden's 51 points
   powered Cleveland past Indiana 128–119 and moved him to third all-time
   in 50-point games. (Reuters, 21 Nov 2026)

## How it runs

`main.py` is standard library only. It copies the `entry.png` shipped in
this repository to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default
`/output/entry.png`). The program takes no network access and writes the
same bytes on every run.

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
